Interviews are a crucial step in any career, but misconceptions often get in the way of good preparation. Here are 20 common myths, and what's actually true.

1. **Myth: Dressing extremely formally guarantees success.** Dress professionally, but match the company's culture. Overdoing it can make you uncomfortable.
2. **Myth: Strong candidates never discuss weaknesses.** Acknowledging a weakness and what you're doing about it shows self-awareness.
3. **Myth: Interviewers only care about skills.** Communication and teamwork often count as much as technical ability.
4. **Myth: Long answers impress.** Clear, concise answers that address the question are more effective.
5. **Myth: The most qualified candidate always gets the job.** Fit, attitude and communication play a big part in hiring decisions.
6. **Myth: Asking questions makes you look uninformed.** Thoughtful questions show interest and preparation.
7. **Myth: Negotiating salary will offend the employer.** Negotiating shows confidence and a realistic sense of your value.
8. **Myth: Silence is always awkward.** A pause can be a moment for thoughtful reflection.
9. **Myth: The interviewer remembers every detail of your resume.** Highlight the key points and be ready to expand on them.
10. **Myth: A thank-you email is optional.** A well-written thank-you shows professionalism and appreciation.
11. **Myth: Humility means underselling yourself.** Humility and confidence work well together.
12. **Myth: You should accept any salary figure they suggest.** Be open to negotiation, and give a range based on your experience and industry standards.
13. **Myth: Interviewers never appreciate humor.** Well-timed, relevant humor can make the conversation more human.
14. **Myth: Preparing too much makes you sound scripted.** Preparation builds confidence and helps you cover the key points naturally.
15. **Myth: Declining an offer burns bridges.** Thank them and give a professional reason, and the relationship can stay intact.
16. **Myth: Technical interviews are only about solving the problem.** How you communicate your thinking matters just as much.
17. **Myth: Showing enthusiasm looks desperate.** Genuine enthusiasm shows interest in the role and the company.
18. **Myth: Jargon impresses interviewers.** Plain, clear language is more effective.
19. **Myth: Online interviews are less formal.** Treat a video interview exactly like an in-person one.
20. **Myth: Following up is annoying.** A polite follow-up restates your interest and professionalism.

## In short

Knowing fact from myth lets you approach interviews with confidence and a clearer sense of what employers actually value.
