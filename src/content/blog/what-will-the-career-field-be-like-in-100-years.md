What will the world of work look like a century from now? Technology, society and global change will shape careers in ways we can only begin to imagine. Here's a speculative look at the year 2123.

## New professions

Advances in artificial intelligence, nanotechnology and bioengineering could create jobs focused on using those technologies ethically and responsibly. AI ethicists, space architects and neural interface designers might become everyday roles.

## Remote work, reimagined

With virtual and augmented reality, colleagues on different continents could work together in immersive shared spaces as if they were in the same room. The traditional office may fade away.

## Sustainability specialists

Climate change will drive demand for people who help us adapt: sustainability specialists, carbon footprint analysts and ecosystem restoration experts.

## Human augmentation and healthcare

Biotechnology could lead to careers in human augmentation, genetic counseling and personalized medicine, from advanced prosthetics to neuroenhancement.

## Entertainment and experience design

As technology redefines reality, virtual reality directors, experience architects and immersive storytellers could create entirely new forms of media.

## Adapting to change

In an era of rapid change, adaptability and lifelong learning will matter most. Moving between professions may become normal.

## In short

No one can predict the future with certainty. What we can do is embrace change, stay curious and keep learning, because the careers of the future will be shaped by people willing to explore the unknown.
